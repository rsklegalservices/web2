Overview & Legal Representation

Criminal proceedings can have serious consequences for an individual’s liberty, reputation, employment and family life. Our advocates assist clients from the initial stage of a criminal complaint or investigation through court proceedings, trial, appeal and other appropriate legal remedies.

Our criminal law practice focuses on understanding the facts of each matter, examining the applicable law and court record, and providing representation appropriate to the stage and nature of the proceedings.

Criminal Defence

Our criminal defence lawyers represent individuals facing criminal allegations and proceedings. Depending on the circumstances, legal assistance may include reviewing complaints and FIRs, advising on available remedies, preparing legal responses, representing clients before investigating authorities and appearing before the appropriate courts.

We assist clients in matters involving allegations under various criminal laws and provide representation throughout the relevant stages of the proceedings.

Bail & Anticipatory Bail

Bail proceedings require prompt legal assistance, particularly where a person has been arrested or apprehends arrest.

Our advocates assist with regular bail, anticipatory bail and other appropriate applications depending on the circumstances of the case. We prepare and present the relevant facts, legal grounds and supporting material before the competent court.

Anticipatory bail may be considered where a person has a reasonable apprehension of arrest in connection with an accusation of having committed a non-bailable offence, subject to the applicable law and circumstances of the case.

Criminal Trials

Criminal trial proceedings involve several stages, including consideration of the prosecution case, framing of charges where applicable, examination and cross-examination of witnesses, presentation of evidence, examination of the accused and final arguments.

Our criminal trial lawyers assist clients throughout the trial process, including preparation of the defence, examination of the prosecution material, cross-examination of witnesses and presentation of appropriate legal arguments.

The approach to each trial depends on the allegations, evidence, procedural stage and applicable provisions of law.

Criminal Appeals

A person aggrieved by a criminal court judgment or order may have a statutory right of appeal, subject to the applicable law and limitations.

Our advocates assist with criminal appeals, including examination of the trial court record, identification of relevant legal and evidentiary issues, preparation of appeal grounds and representation before the appropriate appellate court.

We also advise clients regarding the available appellate remedies and procedural requirements applicable to their particular matter.

Criminal Revisions

Criminal revision proceedings may provide a remedy in circumstances where the applicable statutory provisions permit revision of an order or proceeding.

Our criminal law team assists clients in examining whether a revision petition may be maintainable and, where appropriate, prepares and argues revision proceedings before the competent court.

Each matter is assessed based on the nature of the order, the procedural history and the statutory remedy available.

Quashing Petitions

In appropriate circumstances, proceedings arising from an FIR, complaint or criminal case may be challenged before the High Court under its applicable statutory or inherent jurisdiction.

Our advocates assist clients with quashing petitions, including examination of the FIR, complaint, relevant documents and subsequent proceedings to determine the legal grounds that may be available.

Quashing is not automatic and depends on the facts of the case, the applicable law and the principles governing the jurisdiction of the High Court.

Representation Before Trial Courts

Our criminal defence advocates provide representation before competent Trial Courts in criminal proceedings.

This may include appearances relating to bail, discharge, framing of charges, evidence, cross-examination, examination of the accused, arguments and other applications arising during the proceedings.

Our approach is based on the specific allegations, evidence, procedural history and legal issues involved in each case.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a criminal lawyer do? +

A criminal lawyer advises and represents individuals involved in criminal proceedings. Depending on the case, this may include bail applications, trial proceedings, appeals, revisions, quashing petitions and other criminal law remedies.

When should I contact a criminal defence lawyer? +

It is generally advisable to seek legal advice as early as possible when you become aware of a criminal complaint, FIR, police investigation, arrest, apprehension of arrest or court proceeding. Early advice can help identify the appropriate legal procedure and available remedies.

What is the difference between regular bail and anticipatory bail? +

Regular bail generally concerns release from custody after arrest, while anticipatory bail is a form of pre-arrest protection that may be granted by the competent court in accordance with applicable law where a person apprehends arrest in connection with an accusation.

Can a criminal case be quashed by the High Court? +

In appropriate cases, proceedings may be challenged before the High Court under the jurisdiction available under applicable law. Whether a case can be quashed depends on the facts, allegations, documents and legal principles applicable to the particular matter.

Does PR Sudheer Kumar & Co handle criminal appeals? +

Yes. The firm assists with criminal appellate matters, including examination of the lower court record, preparation of appropriate grounds and representation before the competent appellate court.

Do you represent clients before Trial Courts? +

Yes. Criminal defence representation may include appearances before competent Trial Courts in matters involving bail, evidence, cross-examination, arguments and other stages of criminal proceedings.
