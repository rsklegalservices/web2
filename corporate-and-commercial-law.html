Frequently Asked Questions

What does a corporate lawyer do? +

A corporate lawyer assists companies and businesses with legal matters relating to contracts, corporate documentation, business transactions, disputes, regulatory requirements and other legal issues arising from business operations.

What is the difference between corporate law and commercial law? +

Corporate law generally concerns the legal structure, governance and affairs of companies and other business entities. Commercial law broadly deals with business transactions, contracts, commercial relationships and disputes. There can be significant overlap between the two areas.

Can a corporate lawyer help with business disputes? +

Yes. Corporate and commercial lawyers can assist with disputes involving contracts, shareholders, partners, payments, business transactions and other commercial relationships, depending on the facts and applicable law.

Can you review a business contract before signing? +

Yes. Legal review of a business contract can help identify contractual obligations, risks, termination provisions, liability clauses, dispute-resolution provisions and other important terms before the agreement is executed.

Can you help recover money owed to a business? +

Yes. Depending on the circumstances, legal assistance may include reviewing the underlying documents, issuing a legal notice, negotiating with the concerned party and initiating appropriate recovery proceedings.

Do you handle commercial litigation? +

Yes. We assist with appropriate commercial and corporate litigation before competent courts and forums, depending on the nature and jurisdiction of the dispute.

Can businesses obtain legal advice before a dispute arises? +

Yes. Businesses can seek legal advice regarding contracts, transactions, documentation, regulatory requirements and potential disputes before problems arise. Early legal review may help identify issues and available options.
