Overview & Legal Representation

Businesses often require legal assistance not only when disputes arise, but also when entering into contracts, managing regulatory obligations, structuring transactions or responding to legal issues affecting their operations. Our advocates assist clients with legal review, documentation, dispute management and representation before appropriate courts, tribunals and authorities.

Corporate Advisory

Our corporate legal advisory services assist businesses with legal issues arising from their day-to-day operations and commercial activities.

We provide legal assistance concerning corporate documentation, business arrangements, contractual obligations, transactions and other legal matters affecting companies and business entities.

Our approach is focused on understanding the client's business objectives while identifying relevant legal obligations, risks and available options.

Commercial Contracts

Commercial contracts form an important part of business relationships and transactions.

Our lawyers assist with the drafting, review and legal analysis of commercial agreements, including contracts relating to:

  • Business transactions
  • Services
  • Supply arrangements
  • Consultancy
  • Confidentiality
  • Vendor and customer relationships
  • Commercial engagements
  • Other business arrangements

We assist clients in understanding contractual rights and obligations, identifying potentially problematic provisions and addressing issues arising from breach, termination or non-performance.

Regulatory Compliance

Businesses operate within legal and regulatory frameworks that may vary depending on the nature of their activities, structure and industry.

Our advocates assist clients with legal advice concerning applicable regulatory requirements, notices, compliance-related issues and interactions with relevant authorities.

The scope of regulatory advice depends on the client's business activities and the specific laws and regulations applicable to the matter.

Corporate Disputes

Corporate disputes may arise between shareholders, directors, business partners, companies, contractors, customers or other commercial parties.

We assist clients with disputes involving contractual obligations, business relationships, corporate arrangements and other commercial issues.

Where litigation or another formal remedy is appropriate, our advocates assist with preparation of legal proceedings and representation before the competent court or forum.

Insolvency-Related Matters

Insolvency proceedings involve specific statutory procedures and may affect companies, creditors, financial stakeholders, directors and other parties.

Our lawyers assist clients with legal matters arising in connection with insolvency proceedings, including advice concerning rights, claims, notices, proceedings and related legal remedies.

The appropriate course of action depends on the status of the insolvency proceedings, the parties involved and the applicable provisions of law.

NCLT Matters

The National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT) deals with matters falling within its statutory jurisdiction under applicable company and insolvency laws.

Our advocates assist clients with appropriate NCLT-related proceedings, including matters concerning companies, corporate disputes and insolvency-related issues.

Assistance may include examination of relevant documents, preparation of applications or responses and representation before the appropriate Bench, subject to the jurisdiction and nature of the matter.

Commercial Litigation

Commercial litigation may arise from contractual disputes, business transactions, recovery claims, corporate disagreements and other commercial relationships.

Our commercial lawyers assist businesses with:

  • Contract disputes
  • Recovery proceedings
  • Corporate disputes
  • Commercial claims
  • Injunction proceedings
  • Breach of contractual obligations
  • Other appropriate commercial remedies

We assist clients through the relevant stages of litigation, from assessment of the dispute and preparation of pleadings through hearings and further proceedings where required.

Corporate Legal Documentation

Businesses frequently require legally sound documentation for their commercial activities.

Our advocates assist with the legal drafting and review of appropriate corporate and commercial documents, including agreements, notices, responses and other legal instruments relevant to the client's requirements.

The nature of documentation depends on the transaction, business structure and applicable legal framework.

Corporate Dispute Resolution

Not every commercial dispute necessarily requires prolonged litigation. Depending on the circumstances, parties may consider negotiation, settlement, mediation or other appropriate dispute-resolution mechanisms.

We advise clients regarding available legal options and assist with dispute resolution where appropriate, while preparing for formal proceedings where necessary.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a corporate lawyer do? +

A corporate lawyer provides legal advice concerning businesses and companies, including contracts, corporate arrangements, regulatory issues, transactions, disputes and other legal matters affecting business operations.

Do you review commercial contracts? +

Yes. We assist with legal review and analysis of commercial agreements and advise clients regarding contractual rights, obligations, risks and potential disputes.

Do you handle NCLT matters? +

Yes. We assist with appropriate matters falling within the jurisdiction of the NCLT, including certain corporate and insolvency-related proceedings.

What are insolvency-related legal matters? +

Insolvency-related matters may involve proceedings concerning companies, creditors, claims, corporate obligations and other issues governed by applicable insolvency law. The appropriate legal procedure depends on the circumstances and stage of the proceedings.

Do you handle corporate disputes? +

Yes. We assist with appropriate corporate and commercial disputes involving companies, shareholders, directors, business partners and other commercial parties.

Do you provide regulatory compliance advice? +

We assist with legal issues concerning applicable regulatory requirements and compliance-related matters. The scope of advice depends on the client's business and the specific regulatory framework applicable to it.

Do you handle commercial litigation? +

Yes. Our advocates assist businesses and other commercial clients with appropriate commercial litigation, including contractual disputes, recovery matters, injunction proceedings and other commercial claims.
