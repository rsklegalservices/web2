Overview & Legal Representation

Civil and commercial disputes may involve contractual obligations, recovery of money, property and possession, business relationships, injunctions, specific performance and other legal rights. Our advocates assist clients with legal consultation, drafting, litigation strategy, pleadings and representation before the appropriate courts and forums.

Civil Suits

Civil litigation may arise from disputes concerning property, contracts, possession, money claims, agreements, ownership and other enforceable legal rights.

Our advocates assist clients with the preparation and conduct of civil suits, including examination of relevant documents, identification of legal issues, preparation of pleadings and representation during the proceedings.

The appropriate form of civil proceeding depends on the nature of the dispute and the relief sought.

Commercial Disputes

Commercial disputes can arise between businesses, companies, partners, contractors, suppliers, customers and other parties involved in commercial transactions.

We assist clients in commercial disputes involving contractual obligations, payment issues, business transactions, supply arrangements, service agreements and other commercial relationships.

Our approach focuses on understanding the underlying transaction, contractual obligations, documents and applicable legal remedies.

Contract Disputes

Disputes relating to contracts may involve allegations of non-payment, breach of contractual obligations, delayed performance, defective performance, termination or disagreement regarding contractual terms.

Our lawyers assist clients with reviewing agreements, identifying contractual rights and obligations, issuing legal notices where appropriate and pursuing or defending proceedings arising from contractual disputes.

Recovery Proceedings

We represent clients in proceedings concerning recovery of money and other legally enforceable claims.

Recovery matters may arise from unpaid invoices, loans, commercial transactions, contractual obligations, outstanding payments or other financial claims.

Depending on the circumstances, we assist with legal notices, preparation of recovery proceedings, representation before the competent court and enforcement of applicable orders or decrees.

Injunctions

An injunction may be sought where court intervention is required to prevent a particular act or to protect an existing legal right.

Our advocates assist clients with matters involving temporary or permanent injunctions, including disputes relating to property, possession, contractual obligations and other civil rights.

The availability and scope of injunctive relief depend on the facts of the case and the applicable legal requirements.

Specific Performance

Specific performance is a legal remedy through which a party may seek enforcement of contractual obligations in appropriate circumstances.

Our advocates assist with disputes involving agreements for sale, contractual obligations, property transactions and other matters where specific performance may be available under applicable law.

Each case requires examination of the agreement, conduct of the parties, surrounding circumstances and statutory requirements.

Commercial Litigation

Commercial litigation may involve disputes between businesses, companies, partnerships, contractors and other commercial parties.

Our commercial litigation practice includes assistance with:

  • Contractual disputes
  • Recovery claims
  • Business and transaction disputes
  • Commercial agreements
  • Payment disputes
  • Injunction proceedings
  • Specific performance claims
  • Appeals and other appropriate legal remedies

We assist clients from the initial assessment of the dispute through pleadings, hearings and further proceedings where required.

Civil Appeals

A party aggrieved by a judgment or order may have a statutory right of appeal, subject to the applicable law and procedural requirements.

Our advocates assist with civil appeals by reviewing the trial court record, identifying relevant legal and factual issues, preparing grounds of appeal and representing clients before the competent appellate court.

Commercial Appeals

Commercial judgments and orders may be subject to appellate remedies depending on the nature of the dispute and the applicable statutory framework.

We assist clients with examination of the relevant record, preparation of appellate pleadings and representation in appropriate commercial appellate proceedings.

Arbitration-Related Matters

Where arbitration is actually applicable to a dispute, legal assistance may involve examination of the arbitration agreement, commencement or defence of arbitration proceedings, interim relief and related court proceedings.

PR Sudheer Kumar & Co assists with arbitration-related legal matters where appropriate, subject to the nature of the dispute, the arbitration agreement and the applicable legal framework.

Civil & Commercial Legal Advisory

Not every dispute necessarily requires immediate litigation. In some matters, early legal advice, contractual review, negotiation or a properly drafted legal notice may help clarify the parties' rights and obligations.

Our lawyers assist clients with:

  • Legal review of agreements
  • Contractual advice
  • Legal notices
  • Dispute assessment
  • Pre-litigation strategy
  • Settlement and negotiation
  • Litigation planning
  • Review of available legal remedies

The objective is to help clients understand their legal position and the procedural options available in their particular circumstances.

Representation Before Courts

Our advocates represent clients in appropriate civil and commercial proceedings before the competent courts and forums.

Representation may include drafting pleadings and applications, conducting hearings, presenting legal arguments, examining relevant documents and pursuing appropriate remedies through the applicable judicial process.

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of civil disputes do you handle? +

Civil matters may include property disputes, contractual disputes, recovery claims, injunction proceedings, specific performance matters, possession-related disputes and other civil claims.

Do you handle commercial disputes between businesses? +

Yes. Commercial disputes may include contractual disagreements, payment and recovery disputes, business transaction disputes and other matters arising from commercial relationships.

Can I recover money through a civil proceeding? +

Depending on the nature of the claim and the available documents, a party may pursue appropriate recovery proceedings. The applicable procedure and remedy depend on the circumstances of the transaction and the legal basis of the claim.

What is a suit for specific performance? +

A specific performance proceeding seeks enforcement of a contractual obligation in circumstances where such relief is available under applicable law. Whether the remedy is available depends on the agreement, facts and statutory requirements.

Can I obtain an injunction in a civil dispute? +

An injunction may be available in appropriate circumstances where the legal requirements for such relief are satisfied. The nature of the injunction and the applicable requirements depend on the facts and relief sought.

Do you handle appeals in civil and commercial matters? +

Yes. We assist clients with appropriate civil and commercial appeals, subject to the applicable statutory right of appeal and procedural requirements.

Do you handle arbitration matters? +

We assist with arbitration-related matters where applicable, including legal advice concerning arbitration agreements and related proceedings, depending on the nature of the dispute.
