Appeals & Revisions

PR Sudheer Kumar & Co provides legal assistance in appeals and revision proceedings challenging judgments, decrees, orders and decisions passed by lower courts, Sessions Courts, tribunals and other competent authorities.

Our advocates assist clients in evaluating appellate and revisional remedies, preparing petitions and grounds of challenge, filing and defending appeals, seeking appropriate interim relief and representing clients before the appropriate appellate or revisional courts and forums.

The appropriate remedy depends on the nature of the order or judgment, the court or authority that passed it, the applicable law, limitation requirements and the jurisdiction of the concerned appellate or revisional forum.

Limitation in Appeals & Revisions

Appeals and revisions are subject to applicable limitation periods and procedural requirements.

A delay in filing may affect the availability of a remedy, although the applicable law may provide mechanisms for seeking condonation of delay in appropriate circumstances.

Clients should therefore obtain legal advice promptly after receiving a judgment, decree or order that they intend to challenge.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an appeal? +

An appeal is a legal proceeding through which a party challenges a judgment, decree or order before an appropriate higher court or appellate forum where an appeal is provided by law.

What is a revision petition? +

A revision is a specific legal remedy available in circumstances prescribed by the applicable law. It generally differs from an ordinary appeal in its scope, jurisdiction and grounds.

Can I appeal against every court order? +

No. Not every order is appealable. The availability of an appeal depends on the applicable procedural or substantive law and the nature of the order.

Can I challenge a Sessions Court judgment? +

Depending on the nature of the judgment and applicable law, an appropriate appellate or revisional remedy may be available before the competent court.

Can a tribunal order be challenged? +

Certain tribunal orders may be subject to statutory appeals, review mechanisms or other judicial remedies. The appropriate remedy depends on the governing legislation and jurisdiction.

Can I request a stay while my appeal is pending? +

In appropriate cases, a party may seek interim relief or a stay of the challenged judgment, decree or order. A stay is not automatic and depends on the applicable law and the court's consideration of the application.

How soon should I contact a lawyer after receiving a judgment? +

It is advisable to obtain legal advice promptly because appeals and revisions are subject to limitation periods and procedural requirements.

Can an appeal be defended? +

Yes. A respondent may defend an appeal by responding to the grounds raised by the appellant and presenting the legal and factual position before the appellate court.

What documents are required for filing an appeal? +

Depending on the nature of the matter, documents may include the judgment or order being challenged, pleadings, relevant applications, evidence, previous orders and other case records. The exact requirements vary according to the type of proceeding and forum.
