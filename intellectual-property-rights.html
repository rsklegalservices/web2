Frequently Asked Questions

What does an intellectual property lawyer do? +

An intellectual property lawyer advises and represents clients concerning legal rights relating to trademarks, copyrights, patents and other forms of intellectual property.

What is trademark infringement? +

Trademark infringement generally involves unauthorised use of a protected trademark in circumstances where the applicable legal requirements for infringement are satisfied. The specific legal test depends on the applicable trademark law and facts of the case.

What is passing off? +

Passing off is a common-law remedy concerning misrepresentation that may cause damage to the goodwill or reputation associated with another person's or business's goods or services. The requirements depend on the facts and applicable law.

Can I take legal action if someone copies my copyrighted work? +

Depending on the nature of the work, ownership and circumstances, legal remedies may be available for copyright infringement. The appropriate remedy depends on the applicable copyright law and evidence available.

Can I take action against someone using my brand name? +

Depending on the circumstances, a trademark owner or other rights holder may have legal remedies where another party uses a brand or mark in a manner that infringes applicable rights or gives rise to a passing-off claim.

Do you handle intellectual property litigation? +

Yes. We assist with appropriate trademark, copyright, patent-related, passing-off, brand protection and other intellectual property disputes.

Do you handle online intellectual property disputes? +

Yes. We assist with appropriate disputes involving unauthorised use of trademarks, copyrighted material, photographs, creative content, brands and other intellectual property on websites, social media and online platforms.
