About PR Sudheer Kumar & Co

PR Sudheer Kumar & Co is a law firm providing legal advice, legal drafting, litigation support and representation to individuals, families, businesses, organisations, landowners and other clients across a range of legal matters.

Our legal practice focuses on understanding the facts and legal issues involved in each matter and providing practical legal assistance based on the applicable laws and circumstances of the case. The firm handles matters involving civil law, criminal law, property and land disputes, family law, corporate and commercial law, taxation, intellectual property, cheque bounce cases, writ petitions and other legal proceedings.

The firm also provides legal drafting and documentation services, including legal notices, agreements, petitions, applications, replies, representations and other legal documents.
