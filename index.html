About PR Sudheer Kumar & Co

Advocates & Legal Advisors — Established on Integrity, Diligence & Justice

PR Sudheer Kumar & Co is a full-service law firm with an extensive practice spanning New Delhi, Hyderabad, Kurnool, Vijayawada, and Ananthapur. We offer formidable litigation and advisory services before the Supreme Court of India, the High Court of Andhra Pradesh, the High Court for the State of Telangana, as well as various trial courts and regulatory tribunals across the country.

Our team provides meticulous legal analysis, strategic foresight, and unwavering dedication to protecting our clients' rights and interests in complex civil, criminal, constitutional, and corporate matters.
