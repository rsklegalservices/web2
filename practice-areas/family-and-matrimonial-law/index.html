Overview & Legal Representation

Family disputes can involve sensitive personal, financial and legal concerns. Our advocates assist clients with understanding their legal rights, available remedies and the appropriate procedure before the competent Family Court or other court or authority.

Divorce Proceedings

Divorce proceedings involve the legal dissolution of a marriage under the applicable personal law and statutory provisions.

Our divorce lawyers assist clients with legal consultation, preparation of pleadings, filing of appropriate proceedings and representation throughout the divorce process.

Depending on the circumstances, a divorce may be pursued by mutual consent or through a contested proceeding.

Mutual Consent Divorce

Where both spouses agree to dissolve the marriage, they may pursue divorce by mutual consent subject to the applicable legal requirements.

Our matrimonial lawyers assist with:

  • Understanding the legal procedure
  • Preparation of necessary documents
  • Filing the appropriate petition
  • Representation before the Family Court
  • Matters concerning maintenance and financial arrangements
  • Child custody and visitation arrangements
  • Other mutually agreed terms relevant to the proceedings

The procedure and requirements depend on the applicable law and circumstances of the marriage.

Contested Divorce

A contested divorce may arise where one spouse seeks dissolution of the marriage and the other spouse does not agree to the divorce or disputes the grounds or other issues involved.

Our divorce advocates represent clients in appropriate contested matrimonial proceedings and assist with preparation of pleadings, evidence, applications and legal arguments.

Depending on the facts, matrimonial proceedings may involve allegations and issues relating to cruelty, desertion and other legally recognised grounds for divorce.

Maintenance Proceedings

Maintenance proceedings may arise when a spouse, child or other eligible person seeks financial support under the applicable law.

Our lawyers assist clients in both seeking and responding to maintenance claims, including preparation of appropriate applications, supporting documents and legal submissions.

The entitlement to maintenance and the amount, if any, depend on the applicable law, financial circumstances and facts of the individual case.

Child Custody

Child custody disputes require consideration of the child's welfare and the legal rights and responsibilities of the parents.

Our child custody lawyers assist parents in matters concerning:

  • Custody
  • Visitation and access
  • Parenting arrangements
  • Interim custody
  • Child-related financial arrangements
  • Applications before the Family Court

The appropriate arrangement depends on the circumstances of the child and the applicable legal principles.

Matrimonial Disputes

Matrimonial disputes may involve disagreements between spouses concerning marriage, separation, financial support, property, children and other family matters.

Our matrimonial lawyers provide legal advice and representation in appropriate proceedings and assist clients in understanding the available remedies.

Where appropriate, legal assistance may also involve negotiation or other methods of resolving disputes without prolonged litigation.

Domestic Violence Proceedings

Domestic violence proceedings may involve allegations of physical, emotional, verbal, sexual or economic abuse, depending on the applicable legal provisions.

Our advocates assist clients in appropriate proceedings under the Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act, 2005, including applications for remedies available under the law.

We also represent respondents in proceedings and assist them in understanding and responding to allegations and applications made against them.

The legal remedies available depend on the facts and circumstances of each matter.

Family Court Litigation

Our advocates represent clients in appropriate proceedings before Family Courts and other competent courts.

Family Court matters may include:

  • Divorce proceedings
  • Mutual consent divorce
  • Contested matrimonial proceedings
  • Maintenance claims
  • Child custody matters
  • Visitation and access disputes
  • Matrimonial applications
  • Other family-related proceedings

We assist clients with preparation of pleadings, documentation, court appearances, evidence and legal arguments as required.

Family Dispute Legal Consultation

Family disputes can involve multiple legal issues at the same time. Obtaining legal advice at an early stage can help a person understand the available options and the likely procedural steps.

Our family lawyers assist clients with legal consultations concerning divorce, matrimonial disputes, maintenance, child custody and other family-related matters.

The appropriate legal course depends on the facts, applicable personal law, documents and circumstances of each case.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a family lawyer handle? +

A family lawyer handles legal matters involving marriage, divorce, maintenance, child custody, matrimonial disputes and other family-related proceedings.

What is the difference between mutual consent and contested divorce? +

In a mutual consent divorce, both spouses agree to seek dissolution of the marriage subject to applicable legal requirements. In a contested divorce, the spouses do not have a complete agreement regarding the divorce or issues arising from it, and the matter may require adjudication by the court.

Can I seek maintenance during matrimonial proceedings? +

Depending on the applicable law and circumstances, a spouse or other eligible person may seek maintenance or financial support through appropriate legal proceedings.

How are child custody disputes decided? +

Child custody matters are determined according to the applicable law, with the welfare and interests of the child being an important consideration. The court may consider the circumstances of the child and parents and other relevant factors.

Do you handle domestic violence cases? +

Yes. We assist clients in appropriate proceedings relating to allegations of domestic violence, including representation for persons seeking remedies as well as respondents defending proceedings.

Can matrimonial disputes be settled without a full trial? +

In appropriate circumstances, matrimonial disputes may be resolved through negotiation, mediation or settlement. Whether settlement is appropriate depends on the circumstances and the interests of the parties.

Do you represent clients before Family Courts? +

Yes. Our advocates represent clients in appropriate divorce, maintenance, custody and other matrimonial proceedings before competent Family Courts and courts.
