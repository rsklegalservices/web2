Advocates-on-Record (AOR) & Supreme Court Case Management

PR Sudheer Kumar & Co provides legal assistance in Supreme Court matters through Advocates-on-Record (AOR) and assists clients with the procedural and case-management requirements applicable to proceedings before the Supreme Court of India.

An Advocate-on-Record (AOR) is an advocate recognised under the Supreme Court Rules to act on behalf of a party in the Supreme Court in accordance with the applicable rules. AORs play an important role in filing appearances, pleadings, applications and other documents and in managing the procedural aspects of Supreme Court litigation.

Our lawyers assist with Supreme Court case preparation, AOR coordination, pleadings, applications, filing requirements, case documentation and procedural follow-up, subject to the applicable Supreme Court Rules.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an Advocate-on-Record (AOR)? +

An Advocate-on-Record is an advocate entitled under the Supreme Court Rules to act for a party in proceedings before the Supreme Court in accordance with the applicable rules.

Can any advocate file documents directly in the Supreme Court? +

The Supreme Court Rules prescribe specific requirements concerning who may act and file documents on behalf of parties. An Advocate-on-Record has a specific role in filing and acting for litigants in accordance with those rules.

What does an AOR do in a Supreme Court case? +

An AOR handles procedural and filing-related aspects of a Supreme Court matter, including filing pleadings and applications and complying with applicable Supreme Court Rules and registry requirements.

Can an AOR work with another advocate or arguing counsel? +

Yes. An AOR may coordinate with other advocates, including arguing counsel, in the conduct and preparation of a Supreme Court matter.

Can you assist with Supreme Court SLP filing? +

Yes. We assist with preparation and procedural coordination for appropriate SLP matters, including pleadings, affidavits, supporting documents and filing requirements through the appropriate AOR.

Do you assist with Supreme Court registry objections? +

Our case-management services may include assistance with identifying and addressing filing or registry-related requirements and coordinating compliance with applicable procedural requirements.

Can you manage a Supreme Court case from filing to final hearing? +

Depending on the matter, we can assist with case preparation, documentation, filing coordination, procedural follow-up and coordination with the appropriate AOR and arguing counsel throughout the relevant stages of the proceedings.

How can PR Sudheer Kumar & Co assist with AOR services? +

We assist clients with Supreme Court case preparation, legal documentation, procedural coordination and AOR-related filing requirements in appropriate matters, subject to the Supreme Court Rules and the role of the Advocate-on-Record.
