Overview & Legal Representation

Property disputes can involve title and ownership, possession, partition, boundaries, sale transactions, agricultural land, revenue records and other issues concerning immovable property. Our advocates assist clients with property documentation, legal consultation, dispute resolution and litigation before the appropriate courts and authorities.

Property Disputes

Property disputes may arise between owners, purchasers, family members, co-owners, tenants, occupants or other parties claiming an interest in immovable property.

Our property dispute lawyers assist with matters involving ownership, possession, title, boundaries, agreements, sale transactions and other property-related claims.

We examine the available title documents, agreements, revenue records and other relevant material to advise clients regarding their legal position and available remedies.

Land Disputes

Land disputes can involve ownership, possession, boundaries, access, encroachment, sale transactions and competing claims over land.

Our advocates assist clients in examining land records and relevant documents and provide representation in appropriate civil, revenue and other proceedings.

Each land dispute is assessed based on the nature of the property, documentary records, possession and the legal rights claimed by the parties.

Title & Ownership Issues

A clear examination of title is important when purchasing, selling, transferring or otherwise dealing with immovable property.

We assist clients with legal review of property documents and disputes concerning:

  • Ownership
  • Title
  • Sale deeds
  • Gift deeds
  • Settlement deeds
  • Partition deeds
  • Previous title documents
  • Encumbrances
  • Competing ownership claims

Where a title dispute has already arisen, our advocates can advise on appropriate legal proceedings and remedies.

Partition Matters

Disputes concerning jointly owned or inherited property may require partition to determine the respective shares of the parties.

Our lawyers assist clients with partition-related matters involving family properties, ancestral properties, jointly held properties and other forms of co-ownership.

Depending on the circumstances, assistance may include legal consultation, preparation of notices and pleadings, negotiation between parties and representation in appropriate court proceedings.

Possession Disputes

Possession disputes may arise where a person claims a legal right to occupy or possess a property but another person is in possession or has interfered with that possession.

We represent clients in appropriate proceedings involving possession, unlawful occupation, encroachment and related property rights.

The appropriate remedy depends on the nature of possession, title, documents and circumstances surrounding the dispute.

Injunction Proceedings

An injunction may be sought where court protection is required to prevent interference with property rights or to restrain a particular act.

Our advocates assist with injunction proceedings concerning:

  • Property possession
  • Encroachment
  • Construction disputes
  • Interference with property
  • Boundary-related disputes
  • Sale or transfer-related issues
  • Other circumstances requiring judicial protection

The availability of an injunction depends on the facts and applicable legal requirements.

Revenue Matters

Property ownership and land-related rights may also involve proceedings before revenue authorities.

Our lawyers assist clients with appropriate revenue-related matters concerning land records, mutations, entries, land classification and other issues falling within the jurisdiction of the relevant authorities.

The applicable procedure depends on the nature of the land and the specific revenue issue involved.

Agricultural Land Disputes

Agricultural land may be subject to disputes involving ownership, possession, boundaries, inheritance, partition, cultivation rights, land records and transactions.

We assist landowners and other parties with legal advice and representation in appropriate agricultural land disputes.

Where a matter involves both civil and revenue issues, the legal remedies and appropriate forum are assessed based on the nature of the dispute and applicable law.

Our advocates assist with legal review and preparation relating to property documents, including, where appropriate:

Property Litigation

Property litigation may involve civil suits, injunction proceedings, partition claims, possession disputes, title disputes and other proceedings concerning immovable property.

Our property litigation lawyers assist clients through the different stages of litigation, including assessment of the dispute, preparation of pleadings, filing of appropriate proceedings, hearings, evidence and arguments.

Where an appeal or other legal remedy is available, we also assist clients in pursuing the appropriate proceedings before the competent court.

Property Legal Consultation

We assist clients with property-related legal consultations concerning title, ownership, documents, disputes, possession and available legal remedies.

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of property disputes do you handle? +

Property disputes may include title and ownership disputes, possession matters, partition claims, boundary disputes, encroachment issues, sale-related disputes and other litigation concerning immovable property.

Can a lawyer verify property title? +

A lawyer can examine available title documents, previous transactions and other relevant records to identify apparent legal and documentary issues concerning ownership and title. The scope of verification depends on the documents and records available.

Can I file a case for partition of property? +

A partition proceeding may be available where parties have legally recognised shares or interests in jointly held property. The appropriate remedy depends on the ownership documents, succession and circumstances of the property.

What can I do if someone is interfering with my property? +

Depending on the circumstances, legal remedies may include issuing a legal notice, seeking an injunction or pursuing other appropriate civil or legal proceedings. The correct remedy depends on the nature of the interference and the claimant's legal rights.

Do you handle agricultural land disputes? +

Yes. We assist with appropriate agricultural land disputes involving ownership, possession, boundaries, partition, land records and related civil or revenue proceedings.

Do you handle revenue matters relating to land? +

Yes. We assist with appropriate matters before revenue authorities, including issues concerning land records and other revenue-related proceedings, depending on the nature of the matter and jurisdiction.

Do you provide property documentation services? +

Yes. We assist with legal review and preparation of appropriate property-related documents, subject to the nature of the transaction and the client's requirements.
