Frequently Asked Questions

What types of appeals can be filed before the Supreme Court? +

Depending on the matter, appeals may arise under constitutional provisions, such as Articles 132–134, or under specific statutory provisions. The availability of an appeal depends on the nature of the judgment or order and applicable law.

What is a civil appeal under Article 133? +

Article 133 concerns certain civil appeals to the Supreme Court involving specified requirements, including substantial questions of law of general importance and the relevant constitutional conditions.

What is a criminal appeal under Article 134? +

Article 134 provides for specified criminal appeals to the Supreme Court, subject to the constitutional requirements and applicable procedure.

Can tribunal decisions be appealed? +

Some tribunal decisions can be challenged through statutory appellate mechanisms. The available remedy depends on the governing legislation and the nature of the tribunal's decision.

Can an NCLAT decision be challenged before the Supreme Court? +

Certain NCLAT decisions may be subject to further appeal before the Supreme Court under the applicable statutory framework, subject to the prescribed requirements.

Can an NGT decision be appealed? +

Certain NGT decisions may be challenged through the statutory appellate mechanism provided under the governing environmental legislation, subject to applicable requirements.

Can I seek a stay of the judgment while an appeal is pending? +

A party may seek interim relief or a stay where legally available. Whether relief is granted depends on the facts, applicable law and the decision of the relevant court or tribunal.

What documents are required for filing an appeal? +

Documents depend on the nature of the appeal and forum. They may include the impugned judgment or order, pleadings, evidence, previous orders, relevant records and other documents required under the applicable procedural rules.

How can PR Sudheer Kumar & Co assist with an appeal? +

Our advocates can assist with examining the impugned judgment or order, identifying available appellate remedies, preparing grounds of appeal, drafting and filing the proceedings, preparing interim applications and assisting with appellate representation, subject to the requirements of the particular matter.
