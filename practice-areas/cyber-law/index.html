Frequently Asked Questions

What does a cyber crime lawyer do? +

A cyber crime lawyer provides legal advice and representation in matters involving offences or disputes connected with computers, electronic communications, digital platforms and online transactions.

What should I do if I am a victim of online fraud? +

You should preserve relevant transaction records and digital evidence and promptly report the incident through the appropriate channels. Legal advice can help determine what additional complaints or legal remedies may be available based on the circumstances.

Can I take legal action for online harassment? +

Depending on the conduct involved, legal remedies may be available for certain forms of online harassment, threats, impersonation or other unlawful conduct. The appropriate action depends on the facts and applicable law.

Do you handle cyber fraud cases? +

Yes. We assist with appropriate cyber fraud matters involving online transactions, digital payments, impersonation, phishing and other technology-related allegations.

Do you represent people accused of cyber crimes? +

Yes. We represent persons accused of cyber-related offences in appropriate criminal proceedings, including bail, anticipatory bail, trial and other available legal remedies.

Can digital messages and screenshots be used as evidence? +

Electronic records may be relevant evidence in legal proceedings, subject to the applicable rules governing electronic evidence, authenticity and admissibility.

Can I obtain anticipatory bail in a cyber crime case? +

Depending on the allegations and applicable law, a person apprehending arrest may seek anticipatory bail where such remedy is legally available. The outcome depends on the facts and circumstances of the particular case.
