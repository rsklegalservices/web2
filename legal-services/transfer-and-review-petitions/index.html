Transfer Petitions Under Article 139A

Article 139A of the Constitution of India provides for certain powers of the Supreme Court concerning transfer of cases involving the same or substantially similar questions of law, as well as withdrawal of certain cases in accordance with constitutional requirements.

Our advocates assist with matters involving:

  • Transfer of proceedings between High Courts
  • Proceedings involving common questions of law
  • Transfer-related constitutional matters
  • Consolidation-related proceedings where legally appropriate
  • Withdrawal of cases in circumstances covered by Article 139A

The applicability of Article 139A depends on the facts and constitutional requirements of the particular matter.

Review Petitions

Grounds for Review Petition

The grounds available for review are limited and depend upon the applicable law and procedural framework.

Curative Petitions

A Curative Petition is an exceptional post-judgment remedy recognised by the Supreme Court in limited circumstances after dismissal of a review petition.

Our lawyers assist with examining whether the facts and procedural history potentially fall within the applicable requirements for a curative proceeding.

Because curative jurisdiction is exceptional, the specific requirements and procedural safeguards must be carefully considered before filing.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Transfer Petition? +

A Transfer Petition is a proceeding seeking transfer of a case from one court to another where such transfer is permitted under the applicable law.

What is Article 139A? +

Article 139A of the Constitution provides certain powers to the Supreme Court concerning transfer and withdrawal of cases involving specified questions of law, subject to its constitutional requirements.

Can a case be transferred from one State to another? +

In appropriate circumstances, proceedings may be transferred between courts in different States where the applicable law provides for such transfer and the competent court considers the request.

What is a Review Petition? +

A Review Petition is a limited legal remedy through which a party seeks review of a judgment or order on grounds recognised by the applicable law and procedural rules.

Can a Supreme Court judgment be reviewed? +

The Supreme Court has a limited power to review its judgments and orders subject to constitutional and procedural requirements. Whether review is available depends on the circumstances of the particular case.

What is a Curative Petition? +

A Curative Petition is an exceptional remedy considered by the Supreme Court in limited circumstances after the dismissal of a review petition, subject to the requirements laid down by law and Supreme Court procedure.

Is every judgment eligible for a Curative Petition? +

No. Curative jurisdiction is exceptional and subject to specific requirements. The procedural history and circumstances of the case must be examined before determining whether such a remedy may be available.

Can matrimonial cases be transferred between States? +

In appropriate circumstances, transfer of matrimonial or family proceedings may be sought under the applicable legal framework. The court considers the facts and circumstances relevant to the transfer request.

How can PR Sudheer Kumar & Co assist with Transfer and Review Petitions? +

Our advocates can assist with examining the relevant proceedings or judgment, identifying the applicable legal remedy, preparing transfer, review or curative petitions and supporting documents, and assisting with appropriate proceedings before the Supreme Court.
