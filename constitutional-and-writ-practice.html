Overview & Legal Representation

Constitutional and appellate litigation often involves questions concerning statutory powers, administrative decisions, legal rights, procedural fairness and interpretation of law. Our advocates assist individuals, businesses and other clients with legal consultation, preparation of petitions and appeals, and representation before the appropriate courts.

Constitutional Litigation

Constitutional litigation may involve questions concerning the interpretation and application of constitutional provisions, statutory powers, governmental action and legally protected rights.

Our constitutional lawyers assist clients in identifying the appropriate legal remedy, preparing pleadings and presenting legal arguments before the competent court.

The nature of constitutional proceedings depends on the facts, applicable legislation and constitutional provisions involved in the matter.

Writ Petitions

A writ petition may be filed before a High Court or the Supreme Court in circumstances permitted by the Constitution and applicable law.

Our advocates assist clients with appropriate writ proceedings concerning issues such as:

  • Government and administrative decisions
  • Exercise of statutory powers
  • Procedural irregularities
  • Public authority actions
  • Legal and constitutional rights
  • Challenges to administrative orders
  • Other matters falling within the relevant writ jurisdiction

The maintainability and appropriate form of a writ petition depend on the facts and legal circumstances of the particular case.

High Court Appeals

An appeal provides a statutory remedy in circumstances where the applicable law permits an aggrieved party to challenge a judgment or order.

Our advocates assist with High Court appeals by reviewing the lower court record, identifying relevant questions of law and fact, preparing grounds of appeal and representing clients during appellate proceedings.

The right of appeal and applicable procedure depend on the nature of the original proceeding and the governing legislation.

Appellate Litigation

Appellate proceedings require careful examination of the judgment or order under challenge and the legal grounds available to the appellant or respondent.

Our appellate lawyers assist clients with:

  • Civil appeals
  • Criminal appeals
  • Statutory appeals
  • Challenges to judicial and administrative orders
  • Appellate applications
  • Other appropriate appellate remedies

We assist with preparation of pleadings, examination of the record and presentation of legal submissions before the competent appellate court.

Public Law Matters

Public law matters may involve challenges to decisions or actions of government authorities, statutory bodies and other public authorities.

Our advocates assist clients in appropriate proceedings involving administrative decisions, exercise of statutory powers, public duties and other issues falling within the scope of public law.

Each matter is examined based on the authority involved, the decision under challenge, the applicable legislation and the legal remedy available.

Fundamental Rights Matters

The Constitution of India provides certain fundamental rights and constitutional remedies subject to the applicable constitutional provisions and legal principles.

Our lawyers assist clients in appropriate matters involving alleged infringement of fundamental rights and other constitutional issues.

Depending on the circumstances, constitutional remedies may include proceedings before the High Court or Supreme Court under their respective jurisdictions.

Supreme Court Litigation

The Supreme Court of India exercises constitutional, appellate and other jurisdictions provided by the Constitution and applicable legislation.

Our advocates assist with appropriate Supreme Court matters, including appellate and constitutional proceedings, subject to the jurisdiction and procedural requirements applicable to the particular case.

Where a matter involves a potential Supreme Court remedy, the case requires careful examination of the judgment or order under challenge, the applicable statutory provisions and the available jurisdiction.

Representation Before the Supreme Court

The nature of assistance depends on the case, the applicable remedy and the stage of the proceedings.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is constitutional litigation? +

Constitutional litigation involves legal proceedings concerning constitutional provisions, constitutional rights, governmental powers, statutory interpretation and other issues arising under the Constitution.

What is a writ petition? +

A writ petition is a constitutional remedy available in specified circumstances before the High Courts and the Supreme Court. The jurisdiction, maintainability and appropriate relief depend on the facts and applicable constitutional and legal provisions.

Can I challenge a government order before the High Court? +

In appropriate circumstances, a government or administrative order may be challenged before the High Court through a suitable legal proceeding, including writ jurisdiction where applicable. Whether such a remedy is available depends on the nature of the order, the authority involved and the applicable law.

What is appellate litigation? +

Appellate litigation involves challenging or defending a judgment or order before a higher court where an appeal or other appellate remedy is available under the applicable law.

Do you handle High Court appeals? +

Yes. We assist with appropriate civil, criminal, statutory and other appellate proceedings before the competent High Court.

Do you handle Supreme Court matters? +

We assist with appropriate constitutional and appellate matters falling within the jurisdiction of the Supreme Court, subject to the applicable procedural requirements and nature of the case.

Can fundamental rights violations be challenged in court? +

The Constitution provides remedies for certain violations of fundamental rights. The appropriate remedy and forum depend on the facts and constitutional provisions involved.
