Overview & Legal Representation

Tax disputes may arise from assessments, notices, demands, penalties, searches, investigations, classification issues and other matters under applicable direct and indirect tax laws. Our advocates assist clients with tax-related legal consultation, responses to notices, proceedings before tax authorities, appeals and appropriate litigation before competent courts and tribunals.

Taxation Law

Taxation law involves the legal framework governing the assessment, collection and enforcement of taxes and related obligations.

Our tax lawyers assist clients with legal issues arising under applicable direct and indirect tax laws, including disputes with tax authorities and proceedings arising from assessments, notices and demands.

The applicable legal provisions depend on the nature of the taxpayer, transaction, assessment and tax involved.

Income Tax Matters

Income tax disputes may involve assessments, notices, demands, penalties, deductions, exemptions, classification of income and other issues arising under applicable income tax legislation.

Our advocates assist taxpayers with:

  • Assessment-related proceedings
  • Tax notices
  • Tax demands
  • Penalty proceedings
  • Income classification disputes
  • Deduction and exemption-related matters
  • Appeals
  • Other appropriate tax remedies

Each matter is examined based on the assessment, documents, financial records and applicable provisions of law.

GST & Indirect Tax Matters

Goods and Services Tax and other indirect tax matters can involve registration, classification, input tax credit, assessments, notices, demands, penalties and other compliance or dispute-related issues.

Our lawyers assist businesses and other taxpayers with appropriate GST-related legal matters, including representation in proceedings arising from tax notices, assessments and disputes.

The legal position depends on the transaction, applicable GST provisions, relevant notifications and the facts of the matter.

Tax Notices & Assessments

Tax authorities may issue notices seeking information, clarification, payment or other action from a taxpayer.

Our tax lawyers assist clients in reviewing tax notices, understanding the issues raised and preparing appropriate responses and legal submissions.

Where an assessment or order is adverse to the taxpayer, we advise regarding available statutory remedies, including appeals where applicable.

Tax Appeals

Tax legislation provides specific appellate mechanisms for challenging certain assessments, orders and decisions.

Our advocates assist with appropriate tax appeals by examining the assessment or order, identifying relevant legal and factual issues, preparing grounds of appeal and representing clients before the competent appellate authority or forum.

The right of appeal, limitation period and applicable procedure depend on the nature of the order and governing tax legislation.

Tax Recovery & Demand Disputes

Taxpayers may face demands for payment arising from assessments, reassessments or other proceedings.

We assist clients with legal issues concerning disputed tax demands and available statutory remedies.

Where appropriate, assistance may include challenging the underlying order, pursuing appellate remedies or seeking other relief available under applicable law.

Tax Penalty & Prosecution Matters

Certain tax proceedings may involve penalties or, in specified circumstances, prosecution proceedings.

Our advocates assist clients in appropriate matters involving tax penalty proceedings and related legal issues.

Each matter requires careful examination of the notice, alleged default, applicable statutory provisions and factual circumstances.

Tax Investigation & Search-Related Matters

Tax investigations, surveys, searches and related proceedings can have significant legal and financial implications for taxpayers and businesses.

Our lawyers assist clients with legal advice concerning their rights, obligations and available remedies during or following such proceedings, subject to the applicable law and circumstances.

Where subsequent assessment, penalty or other proceedings arise, we also assist with appropriate legal representation.

Corporate & Business Tax Matters

Businesses may face tax-related legal issues concerning transactions, contracts, payments, business structures and regulatory obligations.

Our advocates assist companies, firms and other business entities with tax-related disputes and legal matters arising from their commercial activities.

The scope of assistance depends on the nature of the transaction, business structure and applicable tax framework.

Tax Litigation

Tax disputes may proceed before statutory authorities, appellate forums, tribunals and courts depending on the nature of the matter.

Our tax litigation practice includes assistance with:

  • Tax assessments
  • Tax appeals
  • GST disputes
  • Tax demands
  • Penalty proceedings
  • Tax recovery matters

We assist clients through the relevant stages of proceedings, including preparation of pleadings, legal submissions and representation before the competent forum.

High Court Tax Litigation

Certain tax disputes may give rise to proceedings before the High Court, subject to the jurisdiction and statutory framework applicable to the matter.

Our advocates assist with appropriate High Court proceedings involving questions of law, jurisdictional issues, procedural concerns and other matters for which High Court jurisdiction is available.

The availability of a High Court remedy depends on the facts, statutory provisions and procedural history of the case.

Tax Legal Notices & Responses

A carefully prepared response to a tax notice can be important in protecting a taxpayer's legal position.

Our lawyers assist with reviewing tax notices and preparing appropriate responses concerning assessments, demands, penalties, GST issues and other tax-related proceedings.

The response is prepared based on the allegations, documents, financial information and applicable law.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a tax lawyer do? +

A tax lawyer provides legal advice and representation concerning tax disputes, assessments, notices, demands, penalties, appeals and tax litigation.

Can a tax notice be challenged? +

Depending on the nature of the notice and applicable law, a taxpayer may have legal remedies available. The appropriate response depends on the contents of the notice, procedural stage and circumstances of the case.

Yes. We assist with appropriate GST-related legal matters, including disputes concerning notices, assessments, demands, penalties and other proceedings.

Do you represent clients in High Court tax matters? +
