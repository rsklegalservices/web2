Overview & Legal Representation

Civil disputes can involve significant financial, property or contractual interests. We assist clients in understanding their legal position, evaluating available remedies, preparing appropriate legal documents and pursuing or defending proceedings based on the facts and applicable law.

Civil Recovery Matters

We assist clients in matters involving recovery of money or other legally recoverable claims.

Injunction Matters

An injunction may be sought where a party requires protection against a particular act or conduct affecting their legal rights or property interests.

Our civil lawyers assist with appropriate temporary and permanent injunction proceedings, including matters concerning property, possession, construction, interference and contractual obligations, subject to the facts and applicable law.

Specific Performance Matters

Where a party seeks enforcement of contractual obligations, specific performance proceedings may be considered where legally maintainable.

We assist clients with disputes involving agreements for sale, contractual obligations, property transactions and other agreements where enforcement of contractual commitments becomes an issue.

Property-Related Civil Disputes

Property disputes may involve ownership, possession, partition, boundaries, agreements, title-related issues or interference with property rights.

Declaration Suits

Where a dispute concerns the legal status or rights of a person in relation to property or another subject matter, a declaratory remedy may be available depending on the circumstances.

We assist clients with preparation and conduct of appropriate declaration proceedings before the competent court.

Partition & Family Property Disputes

Disputes concerning jointly held or family properties may require legal proceedings to determine the respective rights and shares of the parties.

Our advocates assist with partition proceedings, property-related claims, possession issues and related civil litigation, depending on the facts and applicable law.

Our advocates assist with civil appeals, revisions and related proceedings before appropriate appellate courts, subject to the nature of the order, limitation requirements and applicable law.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a civil lawyer do? +

A civil lawyer advises and represents clients in disputes involving civil rights, property, contracts, money recovery, possession, injunctions, specific performance and other non-criminal matters.

When should I consult a civil lawyer? +

It is generally advisable to seek legal advice at an early stage of a dispute, particularly when you receive a legal notice, face a property dispute, have a contractual disagreement or are considering filing or defending a civil case.

Can a civil lawyer help with property disputes? +

Yes. Civil lawyers can assist with appropriate property-related disputes involving ownership, possession, partition, injunctions, declaration and other civil remedies, depending on the facts and applicable law.

Can I send a legal notice before filing a civil case? +

In appropriate circumstances, a legal notice may be sent before commencing litigation. Whether a notice is necessary or advisable depends on the nature of the dispute and the applicable legal requirements.

Do civil lawyers handle appeals? +

Yes. Civil advocates may assist with appeals and other available remedies against judgments or orders, subject to the applicable procedural and limitation requirements.

How do I choose the appropriate civil remedy? +

The appropriate remedy depends on the facts, documents, legal rights involved, limitation, jurisdiction and the nature of the relief sought. A legal consultation can help determine the appropriate course of action.
