Overview & Legal Representation

Employment-related disputes may involve termination, wages, workplace conditions, disciplinary proceedings, employment contracts, employee benefits and other issues arising from the employer-employee relationship. Our advocates assist clients with legal consultation, documentation, dispute resolution, litigation and representation before the appropriate courts, tribunals and authorities.

Employment Law

Employment relationships involve legal rights and obligations for both employers and employees.

Our employment lawyers assist with legal issues arising from employment relationships, including employment contracts, workplace disputes, disciplinary action, termination, employee claims and other employment-related matters.

The applicable legal requirements depend on the nature of employment, establishment, applicable legislation and circumstances of the matter.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a labour lawyer do? +

A labour lawyer provides legal advice and representation concerning employment and workplace disputes, including termination, wages, disciplinary proceedings, employment contracts and other labour-related matters.

Can an employee challenge termination? +

Depending on the nature of employment, applicable law and circumstances of termination, an employee may have legal remedies available to challenge or seek relief concerning termination. The available remedy depends on the individual facts and applicable legislation.

Can an employee claim unpaid salary? +

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, an employee may have legal remedies for unpaid salary, wages or other employment-related dues.

Do you represent employers as well as employees? +

Yes. We assist both employers and employees in appropriate labour and employment matters, including disputes, legal notices, disciplinary proceedings and litigation.

Do you handle disciplinary proceedings? +

Yes. We assist employers and employees with appropriate legal issues arising from disciplinary proceedings, including notices, responses and related legal proceedings.

Can employment disputes be settled without litigation? +

Depending on the circumstances, employment disputes may be resolved through negotiation, conciliation, mediation or settlement. The appropriate approach depends on the nature of the dispute and the interests of the parties.

Do you handle Labour Court matters? +

Yes. We assist clients with appropriate proceedings before competent labour courts, tribunals and other employment-related forums, subject to the jurisdiction and nature of the dispute.
