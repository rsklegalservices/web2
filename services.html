Core Services Offered

  • •
    Writ Petitions: Filing and defending petitions (like Habeas Corpus, Mandamus, or Certiorari) to protect fundamental rights or challenge government and administrative actions.
  • •
    Appeals and Revisions: Challenging orders, decrees, or judgments passed by lower courts, sessions courts, or tribunals.
  • •
    Criminal Proceedings: Handling anticipatory bail applications, regular bail, criminal revisions, and petitions to quash FIRs or criminal proceedings.
  • •
    Civil and Commercial Litigation: Managing high-stakes property disputes, money recovery suits, injunctions, and contractual disputes.
  • •
    Service and Employment Matters: Representing government or private employees in disputes involving wrongful termination, suspensions, transfers, promotions, and pension issues.
  • •
    Tribunal and Corporate Representation: Appearing before specialized bodies like the National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT), Debt Recovery Tribunal (DRT), or the Central Administrative Tribunal (CAT).
  • •
    Legal Drafting and Opinion: Preparing complex legal notices, petitions, affidavits, counter-affidavits, and providing formal opinions on intricate points of law.
  • •
    Special Leave Petitions (SLPs): Filing and arguing petitions under Article 136 to challenge judgments from lower courts or High Courts.
  • •
    Writ Petitions: Filing constitutional remedies under Article 32 for the enforcement of fundamental rights.
  • •
    Appeals: Managing civil appeals (Articles 132–133), criminal appeals (Article 134), and statutory appeals from national tribunals like the NCLAT or NGT.
  • •
    Transfer and Review Petitions: Handling requests to transfer cases between courts in different states (Article 139A) or seeking reviews/curative actions of prior apex court judgments.
  • •
    Case Management via Advocates-on-Record (AOR): Only certified AORs are permitted to file appearances, pleadings, or act for litigants in the Supreme Court.
