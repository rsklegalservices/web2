Legal Drafting & Legal Opinion

PR Sudheer Kumar & Co provides professional legal drafting and legal opinion services for individuals, businesses, organisations, and other clients requiring carefully prepared legal documents and considered advice on complex legal issues.

Our advocates assist with the preparation of legal notices, petitions, affidavits, counter-affidavits, applications, replies, representations, agreements and other legal documents. We also provide formal legal opinions on intricate points of law, contractual issues, property matters, civil and commercial disputes, and other legal questions, depending on the facts and applicable law.

Legal Drafting Lawyers

Legal drafting requires accuracy, clarity and a proper understanding of the facts, applicable legal provisions and relief sought. Our lawyers assist clients in preparing legally structured documents intended for use before courts, tribunals, government authorities, businesses and private parties.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is legal drafting? +

Legal drafting is the preparation of legal documents in a structured and legally appropriate form. It may include notices, petitions, affidavits, agreements, applications, replies and other documents.

What is a legal opinion? +

A legal opinion is a formal assessment of a particular legal issue based on the facts, documents, applicable law and relevant legal principles. The scope of an opinion depends on the specific matter and information provided.
